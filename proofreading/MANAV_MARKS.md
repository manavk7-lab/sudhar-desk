## What this teaches
- Commas are his most common change (about 140 added, 12 removed). He adds them: between two clauses of one sentence
  ("… છે, જે …", "… નથી, એ …", "… હોય, તો …"); in lists and repetitions ("ઓમ, ઓમ, ઓમ"); after a vocative
  ("હે મહામુનિ, …"); around a word the speaker adds to explain ("સંપૂર્ણ, પૂર્ણતા"); before a short question tag
  ("…, કેમ?", "…, ભાઈ?").
- Questions: he adds "?" wherever the speaker asks — also short ones ("બરાબર?", "કેવી રીતે?", "ક્લિયર?") — and turns
  "." or "," into "?" at the end of a question.
- Stops: most "." changes are MOVES — the stop removed and put one or two words later, over a trailing word that finishes
  the sentence ("… નથી એમ. ઘણા …").
- Dashes "–" where the speaker points to what follows ("ઇક્વિવેલેન્ટ ટુ – અ", "આ લાલ છે – અ"); ";" between two closely
  joined thoughts.
- In passages he READS from the book, Manav wants the book's own punctuation (15 Nov) — t/bookpunct.py copies it after
  this pass.
