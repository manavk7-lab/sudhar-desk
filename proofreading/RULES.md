# Proofreading rules — શાશ્વત સંવાદ transcripts (Manav's style)

These rules are learned from how Manav corrects the transcripts by ear: YouTube captions vs his finished
Yatharth Gita videos, શાશ્વત સંવાદ 1–7, and his own pass over શાશ્વત સંવાદ 9 (after the Sarvam + Gemini pass).
Every correction pass (human, AI or speech-recogniser fusion) must follow them.

The goal is the speaker's exact words, not "better Gujarati". When in doubt, keep what was said.

## 1. Keep the spoken forms (never "standardise")
- કે છે (= "says") stays **કે**. Do not write કહે છે when he said કે છે.
- તું / તી / તા (= was) stay. Don't change them to હતું / હતી / હતા. Also keep રે (= stay), આયા / આયો, જાવ / થાવ, બી, કોક, ભઈ, તઈ, દાડો, જરાક, જોવે, લેવાઈ, આઈતી.
- The Surti હ for સ stays: હવાર, હમજો, હમજાયું, હંભળાય.
- English words stay as he says them: ક્લોરોફોમ (not ક્લોરોફોર્મ), ટ્રેસપાસિંગ.
- Numbers stay as written (25, 33, 72). Don't convert digits to words.
- Repeats the speaker means stay: ધીમે ધીમે, આમ આમ, બધી બધી, હવે હવે.
- **હમ્મ** stays where he says it.
- **આગળ / આગળે**: the speaker says both. In video 9 Manav changed one અહીંયા આગળ to **આગળે** and left four ત્યાં આગળ alone. Change it only when the audio has આગળે.

## 2. Spellings he always fixes
નહી→**નહીં**, કઈ/કાઈ→**કંઈ**, કઈક→**કંઈક**, અહી→**અહીં**, અહિયા→**અહીંયા**, છતા→**છતાં**, ક્યાય→**ક્યાંય**,
નિરાતે→**નિરાંતે**, ઉભો→**ઊભો**, બેહી→**બેસી**, બેહાડી→**બેસાડી**, હારું/હારો/હાચી→**સારું/સારો/સાચી**, ખયાલ→**ખ્યાલ**,
હરગેજ→**હરગીજ**. A cut-off word gets its full form: પછ→**પછી**, તમ→**તમે**.

## 3. એણે, not એને, when "he/she DID it" (new, from video 9)
Recognisers and the AI cleaner write **એને** where the speaker says **એણે**. They sound almost the same.
- If એ / આ / તે / જે is the **doer** of a past action (કર્યું, કીધું, કહ્યું, આપ્યું, જોયું, લીધું, દીધું, માર્યું, ખાધું …), write **એણે / આણે / તેણે / જેણે**. This only applies when there is no other doer in the clause.
  - રતિને શિવજીએ બાળી નથી. **એણે** શું કર્યું? કામદેવનું દહન કરી દીધું.
  - **આણે** મને જે જે ના કર્યા …
- Keep **એને** when it means "to him / him":
  - યમીએ ત્યાં આગળ **એને** કીધું
  - તમે **એને** આપી દીધું
  - મેં **એને** કીધું
  - **જેને** કહ્યું (= "which is called")

## 4. The speaker's "શું?" habit (new, from video 9)
The speaker often throws in a lone **શું?** between sentences, e.g. "…તોડી નાખશે. શું? ક્રોધ, માન, …".
Manav deleted every one of these (8 out of 8) in video 9, and 5 other filler શું as well.
- Delete a lone શું? that stands as its own sentence between two sentences.
- Never add a rhetorical શું. Real questions stay: એટલે શું? / શું કરવાનું? / તો શું થયું?

## 5. Mantras and shlokas: the standard text, taken from the internet (rule from 29 Oct 2026)
Manav: "always remember mantra should be replaced by getting from internet". Unlike everything else in this file, a mantra
is NOT written the way the speaker pronounces it.
- When the speaker recites a mantra, shloka or verse (shanti path, Gita, Upanishad, Gayatri, stotra), look it up in a
  reliable source online and replace the transcript's version with that standard text, in Gujarati script with the proper
  Sanskrit spelling: anusvara ં (ભદ્રં), visarga ઃ (દેવાઃ, શાન્તિઃ), halant ્ (સૃજામ્યહમ્), words joined as printed
  (પશ્યેમાક્ષભિર્યજત્રાઃ).
- Layout as printed, like Manav's own correction of the shanti path in ઉપનિષદ - 1: the mantra starts on a new line, one
  printed line per line, "।" at the end of a line and "॥" at the end of a verse. The speaker's Gujarati carries on after it.

      ૐ ભદ્રં કર્ણેભિઃ શૃણુયામ દેવાઃ ।
      ભદ્રં પશ્યેમાક્ષભિર્યજત્રાઃ ।
      સ્થિરૈરંગૈસ્તુષ્ટુવાંસસ્તનૂભિઃ ।
      વ્યશેમ દેવહિતં યદાયુઃ ॥
      સ્વસ્તિ ન ઇન્દ્રો વૃદ્ધશ્રવાઃ ।
      સ્વસ્તિ નઃ પૂષા વિશ્વવેદાઃ ।
      સ્વસ્તિ નસ્તાર્ક્ષ્યો અરિષ્ટનેમિઃ ।
      સ્વસ્તિ નો બૃહસ્પતિર્દધાતુ ॥
      ૐ શાન્તિઃ શાન્તિઃ શાન્તિઃ ॥

  (It had been "ૐ ભદ્રમ કર્ણેભિહ શૃણુયામ દેવાહ, ભદ્રમ પશ્યેમાક્ષભી યત્રાહ. … ૐ શાંતિ શાંતિહ શાંતિહી.")
- Write only what the speaker recites. If he says half a verse, write that half in the standard text; don't add lines he
  skipped, and keep his own Gujarati words in between (આગળ કહે છે, …).
- A short mantra phrase inside a Gujarati sentence gets the standard spelling where it stands (તત્સવિતુર્વરેણ્યં,
  હરિ ૐ તત્ સત્).
- Only the recited Sanskrit changes. The Gujarati translation or explanation around it stays as spoken, and so do Sanskrit
  words used inside a Gujarati sentence (ભૂ સ્વરૂપ, ભુવ સ્વરૂપ).
- If no reliable source can be found, leave the line as it is and put it on the ✎ list to check.
- This replaces the old rule "Sanskrit as pronounced" (video 9: સદગુરુ→સદગુરુમ, ગુરુ→ગુરુમ, ભવે→ભવેત, …): verses like
  those are now written in their standard form too (… સદ્ગુરું તં નમામિ).

## 6. Small words that get dropped — put them back
Most common, in order: **છે**, એ, **કે**, **ય**, છું, છો, છીએ, નથી, જ, તો, કહે, પણ, અને.
ય comes either as a separate word (એ ય, એટલે ય, આવતાં ય, સહેજે ય) or joined (ત્યારેય, પાંચેય, સદાય, આખાય, તેમાંય, એનાથીય).
Unfinished verbs get their helper back:
- થઈ→થઈ **ગયું**
- કરી→કરી **શક્યો**
- થતું→થતું **નથી**
- ના→ના **થાય**
- લાગે?→લાગે **છે**?
- સ્વરૂપ.→સ્વરૂપ **છે**.

Add one only when the sentence is clearly incomplete without it, or a recogniser heard it.

## 7. Words to delete
- Fillers: હેને, હને, હ, હેં, હેંને, [હાસ્ય]. Also હે when it's a filler; keep હે when it means "O …".
- The lone શું? (section 4).
- Stutter fragments the speaker didn't mean (a half word followed by the whole word).

## 8. Real mishearings — fix to the word that fits
Some examples Manav fixed:
- ખાદા→ખાધા, પડોજનમાં→પળોજણમાં, જીજી વિસા→જીજીવિષા
- ટવાઈ→અટવાઈ, નમે→નથી, પછોડ→પછી છોડી
- એને મેલ મેલ→એની મેળે મેળે, કહેવું→કેવું (= "how")
- પરણ્યાને લાવો ને→પરણેલાઓને, નથી કહેતા→નચિકેતા, અડચણ રહી ગયો→અવળચંડાઇઓ

The topic (Vedanta, Sadguru teaching), the grammar and the sentence tell you the right word.

## 9. Punctuation — full stops by pause AND meaning
Manav: "when it is pause then it should be dot … but not always — apply your mind".
Measured in his finished video 9 (0–1:34), with the pauses taken from the audio:
- Where the speaker pauses ≥ 0.5 s, he has a full stop 55%, a comma 17%, nothing 28% → a pause alone is not a full stop.
- Full stops he removed had no pause (79%); full stops he added sat at a pause (median 0.8 s).
- His typical change is a **move**: the speaker says a few trailing words after the verb and only then pauses, so the stop
  goes after them: "રાહ જોશે. પછી ફરી" → "રાહ જોશે પછી. ફરી", "દીધી. જાણે આવી" → "દીધી જાણે. આવી",
  "બેસવાનું. આપણે બેસવું" → "બેસવાનું આપણે. બેસવું".
- He keeps a full stop between two complete sentences even when the speaker runs them together, and he almost never adds commas.
- The automatic pass (pause from the audio + reading the sentence, see PUNCT.md) matched his own choice about 3 times in 4
  on a part it had not seen, and found about a third of his punctuation changes. On video 11 (which he finished himself) it did
  worse: 13 of 31 changes exactly his, 18 of 31 at a spot he also changed — most misses were stops moved over તો / આપણે / પછી,
  so the pass now leaves those alone (PUNCT.md). Its changes are on the ✎ list ("dot moved",
  "dot added at a pause") so a wrong one can be undone with Alt+Backspace.
When typing, keep one space after . , ? ! — in video 9, 36 edits ended up as "અંદર.ભલે" or "નથી.તે".

## 10. How the speech-recogniser pass decides (for AI passes)
- **S** = Sarvam, **G** = Gemini, **Y** = YouTube captions. The Doc was made from Y, so Y agreeing with the Doc proves nothing.
- S and G agree, differ from the Doc, and their word fits the sentence → fix. In video 9, Manav kept 97% of these changes.
- Only one of S or G differs → the Doc is usually right. In video 9, Gemini alone was right in 4% of its differences and Sarvam alone in 2%. Fix only when the Doc's word makes no sense.
- Both recognisers standardise speech (કે→કહે, તું→હતું, જાવ→જાઓ, digits→words) and drop repeats. Never copy that.
- Doubtful spots go on the ✎ list as "listen" items, not into the Doc.

## 11. Lessons from Manav's own pass over videos 9 (1:34–end), 10, 11, 12 and 13 (checked 29 Oct)
He kept the changes from the Sarvam + Gemini pass: of the 1,159 changes that can still be traced, he undid 19 (about 2%).
What he still corrected after the pass, most common first:
- **ય written as એ.** When a lone એ inside a sentence means "also / even", it is **ય** (a separate word). The recognisers
  write એ here too, so decide by meaning — if એ cannot be "he / she / that" in the clause, it is ય:
  - તમારાથી એ બહુ ભૂલો થઈ છે → તમારાથી **ય** બહુ ભૂલો થઈ છે
  - તમારા શરીરમાંથી એ કશું વ્યય નહીં થાય → શરીરમાંથી **ય** કશું
  - સંભળાવનારો એ અંદર → સંભળાવનારો **ય** અંદર · થોડું ઘણું એ અજવાળું → થોડું ઘણું **ય** અજવાળું
  He also added ય where nothing was written: ત્યાં જઈને **ય** માળા કરે, આ **ય** એની કૃપા છે, રાત્રે તો એ **ય** બધા.
- **એમણે, not એમને**, when they are the doer (section 3 applies to the plural too): એટલે **એમણે** કીધું, **એમણે** તો કરાવી
  દીધું, **એમણે** શું કર્યું. Same for તેમણે / જેમણે / આમણે.
- **Lone શું** — he deletes it with or without "?", and after a comma too: "કાઢ્યું કે, શું? ઘસી ઘસીને",
  "બેસવાનું. શું આનંદ કરવાનો …", "ગમે તેમ કરીને, શું જોગવાઈ કરવી પડશે". A real question stays (શું કીધું? … answered next).
- **Fillers**: a closing **હા** (થઈ જશે હા. → થઈ જશે.) and the English **સો** (= "so") opening a sentence
  (છે. સો વ્યાપકમ્ → છે. વ્યાપકમ્).
- **Stutters**: never put back a repeated word just because all three recognisers heard it (હું હું, છે છે, ખબર છે ખબર) —
  he removed those. Repeats the speaker means stay (ધીમે ધીમે, વહે જાય, વહે જાય).
- **The speaker's closing કે / કે છે** (= "he says") at the end of a sentence gets dropped: ભાઈ **કે.**, હાજર રહેજે **કે.**,
  ગયો નથી **કે છે.** Put it back when a recogniser heard it.
- **કોક**, not કોઈ, where the speaker says કોક.
- **No Sanskrit endings on names or Gujarati words**: he took the મ back off સંતરામ (a name) and સર્વજ્ઞ (દાદા ભગવન
  સર્વજ્ઞ શરણમ ગચ્છામિ). For whole mantras and shlokas, section 5 now applies (standard text from the internet).
- Mishearings he fixed: વહી→વહે, મામૂલી→મહામૂલી, શીદ→શીત, ભાગી→ભાંગી, સ્વપ્ન→સ્વપ્નું, જોઈતી→જોઈએ, સેજ→સહેજ,
  સાધનસાધ્ય→સાધન સાધ્ય, ઉપર થઈ→ઉપરથી, આગળ→આગળે (ત્યાં આગળે).
- He also removes asides that are not part of the talk (a remark to someone in the room).
